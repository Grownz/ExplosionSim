Kenney Car Kit
==============

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The .esv files in this folder are converted from the pack's OBJ models by
`scripts/convert-kenney-vehicles.py`. Each face is flat-shaded and its colour
is sampled from the pack's `colormap.png`; no textures are required at runtime.

Models used:
- sedan
- sedan-sports
- hatchback-sports
- suv
- suv-luxury
- van
- taxi
- police
- truck
- truck-flat

CC0 does not require attribution, but the source is credited here and in the
project README.
